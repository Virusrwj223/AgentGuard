Read the approved pack and the proposed scenario. Trace forward from each operation and backward from loss, refund, liability, availability and retention. Check which outcomes are conditional, whether state observes the claimed condition, whether refunds accrue twice and whether every success, failure or decline terminates. Record a specific affected operation, severity (material or minor), source and proposed correction within the finding. Submit one pending gap per issue with submit_risk_review. A founder may approve a material gap to suspend the affected operation. Do not change the pack or issue an offer. State the horizon and unexamined contexts.
