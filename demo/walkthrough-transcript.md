# AgentGuard local model walkthrough

## A change before a commitment

A traveller wants to move an existing booking thirty days later at no extra cost. For a small travel agency, an instant yes might buy goodwill, but it can also create an obligation before the supplier agrees. This walkthrough follows that request through the AgentGuard implementation. Northline Travel and every financial figure in this example are fictional.

## What the sales agent can propose

The sales skill gives QwenWork a narrow job. It reads the current booking and proposes alternatives as structured data. The compiler obtains prices, probabilities and risk preferences from approved records. A session scoped connector keeps the booking identity under backend control. The bundled replay supplies a labelled proposal for this walk through. A live QwenWork session remains a separate integration step.

## Where the rare loss enters

The guarantee has three outcomes. A successful amendment costs eighty dollars. A failed amendment costs one hundred and fifty dollars to recover. In one percent of those failures, an additional claim costs one million dollars. Because failure itself occurs twenty percent of the time, the joint claim probability is two tenths of one percent. The compiler preserves that dependency.

## One query for all strategies

Each terminal path has a monetary value, which the owner profile converts to utility. The compiler maps that number into a synthetic final probability and emits the same reachability assertion for every alternative. The synthetic event has no commercial meaning. It lets the program reconstruct expected utility from the result of model checking. A separate exact rational calculation checks the arithmetic.

## The value of waiting

The reference comparison shows the effect. Declining costs one hundred and fifty dollars in expected repeat business. Guaranteeing the date costs two thousand and ninety four dollars in expected value. Waiting for a binding hold costs ninety four dollars, a fifty six dollar improvement over declining. Under the approved adverse scenario, the conditional strategy still improves on the baseline by forty nine dollars.

## What the customer can accept

The development preview stops here and sends the request for review. On a configured PAT host, a qualifying decision can issue a short lived authorization. The customer then sees the exact terms. The executor checks the session, terms, snapshot and expiry in one transaction. It changes a contingent booking only after the mock supplier returns a binding hold. A stale or repeated authorization is blocked.

## Reconstruct the decision

The founder can inspect each candidate, read its model, and trace a financial consequence to an approved assumption. Updating an estimate creates a new pack version and invalidates stale approvals. Sixteen labelled structured-plan cases exercise the downstream workflow. This recording uses the exact preview. A deployed demonstration still needs a genuine PAT run, a QwenWork session and a published Hashmeta site.
